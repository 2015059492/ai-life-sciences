AI Integration in Life Sciences Teaching Course

This self-paced course is designed to help South African Grade 10–12 Life Sciences teachers confidently integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI) into their teaching practice. You do not need advanced technical skills — the course focuses on simple, practical ways AI can support your teaching, save time, and enhance learner understanding. Through short lessons, real classroom examples, and reflection activities, you will explore how AI can help with content creation, inquiry-based learning, data analysis, lesson planning, and differentiated instruction. The course also includes guidance on responsible and ethical use of AI in schools. By the end of the course, you will be able to design AI-supported Life Sciences lessons, use AI tools meaningfully within CAPS requirements, and complete a final exam to earn your Professional Certificate in AI Integration for Life Sciences Teaching. This course is flexible, mobile-friendly, and suitable for both beginners and experienced digital users. Whether you teach in a well-resourced school or a low-tech environment, the tools and strategies shared in this course will help make your teaching more engaging, efficient, and future-focused.

Course Content

Module 1: Introduction to AI

3 lessons

Lesson 1

In this lesson, you will get a simple and clear understanding of what Artificial Intelligence (AI) is. Many teachers hear about AI but still feel unsure about what it actually means or how it works. This lesson explains AI in everyday language and shows how you are already using AI in your daily life—often without realising it. This basic understanding will help you feel more confident as we move into AI for teaching.

Lesson 2

In this lesson, we look at why AI is becoming relevant in Life Sciences education. Many teachers feel that AI is only for computer scientists, but in reality, AI is already being used in medicine, genetics, environmental research, and many Life Sciences careers. Understanding this connection helps teachers make their lessons more engaging and aligned with real-world developments.

Lesson 3

Many teachers feel nervous about using AI because they think it is something new or complicated. But the truth is: most teachers already use AI every day without calling it “AI.” This lesson helps you recognise familiar tools that use AI in the background. Once you realise you are already using AI, it becomes easier to understand how it can fit into your teaching.

Module 2: AI Tools for Life Sciences Teaching

2 lessons

Lesson 1

In this lesson, you will explore simple AI tools that can help you create teaching materials more quickly. These tools can help you generate notes, explanations, worksheets, and examples for your Life Sciences lessons. You do not need advanced skills — you just type what you need, and the AI supports you. This can save time and help you create clearer, more engaging content for your learners.

Lesson 2

In Life Sciences, learners often work with data, observations, diagrams, and investigations. AI tools can support these activities by helping teachers and learners analyse information, interpret data, and explore scientific ideas. This lesson introduces simple AI tools that make inquiry-based learning easier, even in classrooms with limited resources.

Module 3: Designing AI-Enhanced Lessons (Using TPACK & UDL Simply)

2 lessons

Lesson 1

In this lesson, you will learn how to design lessons that include AI tools in a meaningful and practical way. You do not need to be an expert in any framework. We will use simple explanations of TPACK and UDL to help you think about how content, teaching strategies, and technology can work together to support your Life Sciences lessons.

Lesson 2

In this lesson, you will put everything you have learned into practice by designing your own AI-enhanced lesson or classroom activity. Using simple prompts and templates, you can create materials that support your learners’ understanding and engagement. No advanced skills are needed — just your knowledge of Life Sciences and the tools introduced earlier.

Module 4: Responsible and Ethical AI Integration

2 lessons

Lesson 1

AI is a powerful tool, but it comes with ethical considerations that teachers need to understand. In this lesson, you will learn about key ethical issues in AI, including bias, privacy, and copyright, and why it’s important to use AI responsibly in your classroom.

Lesson 2

AI is a powerful tool, but it comes with ethical considerations that teachers need to understand. In this lesson, you will learn about key ethical issues in AI, including bias, privacy, and copyright, and why it’s important to use AI responsibly in your classroom. Understanding these concepts ensures that AI becomes a safe, supportive tool rather than a risk.

Module 5: Final Exam

1 lesson

Take Exam

Congratulations! You have reached the final part of this course. In this module, you will demonstrate your understanding of AI integration in Life Sciences teaching by completing a final project/exam. The project will ask you to create a lesson plan or activity that integrates AI responsibly, aligned with CAPS and ethical guidelines. This is your opportunity to apply everything you’ve learned in a real teaching scenario.
