Lesson 1

Module 2: AI Tools for Life Sciences Teaching

In this lesson, you will explore simple AI tools that can help you create teaching materials more quickly. These tools can help you generate notes, explanations, worksheets, and examples for your Life Sciences lessons. You do not need advanced skills — you just type what you need, and the AI supports you. This can save time and help you create clearer, more engaging content for your learners.

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, teachers will be able to:

  1. Identify AI tools that support inquiry and data analysis.

  2. Explain how these tools can be used in Life Sciences lessons.

  3. Use AI to help learners explore scientific questions and data.

1. What Are Inquiry and Data-Focused AI Tools?

These tools help teachers and learners:

  • analyse data

  • interpret graphs

  • explore patterns

  • run simple simulations

  • ask scientific questions

  • visualise concepts

You do not need advanced math or programming. You simply upload or describe the data, and the AI helps interpret it.

2. Helpful AI Tools for Inquiry and Data Analysis

1. ChatGPT or Gemini with Data Upload

These tools can analyse simple data tables or graphs.

Examples:

  • Upload a table of plant growth data and ask:
    “What pattern do you see in the data?”

  • Give a graph description and ask:
    “Write two questions learners can answer from this graph.”

Good for:

  • ecology

  • photosynthesis experiments

  • human physiology investigations

2. PhET Simulations (OER)

While not AI-based, they are a useful digital tool that supports inquiry.

Examples:

  • simulations on diffusion, circuits, ecosystems

  • learners explore “what happens if…”

AI Tip: You can ask an AI tool to help create questions about the simulation.

3. Google Lens or Image AI Tools

Helpful for quick identification or explanation.

Examples:

  • identify plant leaves

  • analyse microscope images

  • compare organisms

This supports observational skills in Life Sciences.

3. How AI Supports Inquiry-Based Learning

AI tools can help learners:

  • generate hypotheses

  • interpret results

  • explain trends or anomalies

  • design simple investigations

  • check their thinking

  • compare different ideas

For example, learners can ask:
“What could cause a decrease in plant growth in this experiment?”

AI does not replace scientific thinking — it guides and supports it.

4. Using AI in Low-Resource Contexts

In schools where learners do not have devices, teachers can still use AI to:

  • prepare data sets for lessons

  • generate sample investigation questions

  • create step-by-step explanations

  • produce visual aids for practical work

  • simulate simple experiments digitally when lab resources are limited

This helps bridge gaps in resource availability.

  • OER: PhET Interactive Simulations (useful for Life Sciences inquiry)

Reflection Question

How could AI support learners in understanding or interpreting scientific data in one of your Life Sciences topics?
