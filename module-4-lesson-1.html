Lesson 1

Module 4: Responsible and Ethical AI Integration

AI is a powerful tool, but it comes with ethical considerations that teachers need to understand. In this lesson, you will learn about key ethical issues in AI, including bias, privacy, and copyright, and why it’s important to use AI responsibly in your classroom.

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, teachers will be able to:

  1. Explain basic ethical concerns related to AI use.

  2. Recognise potential risks of AI in education.

  3. Understand how to verify AI-generated content.

Privacy and Data Security

  • AI systems often collect and process data.

  • Personal data of learners must be protected.

  • Avoid uploading learner names, photos, or sensitive data into AI platforms without permission.

  1. Bias in AI

    • AI can reflect existing social, cultural, or gender biases from the data it was trained on.

    • For example, an AI-generated assessment might favour certain groups unintentionally.

    • Teachers should review AI content carefully to ensure fairness.

  2. Copyright and Intellectual Property

    • AI may generate content based on existing work.

    • Always check that materials used comply with copyright rules.

    • Encourage learners to cite sources even if AI generates the content.

  3. Verifying AI Content

    • AI is not always correct.

    • Always cross-check facts, especially for scientific information.

    • Example: If AI gives a plant classification, verify using a textbook or trusted online source.

  4. Why It Matters for Teachers

    • Ensures safe and responsible AI use.

    • Sets an example for learners about ethical digital behaviour.

    • Prevents legal or reputational issues in schools.

Reflection Question
Think about one way AI could unintentionally cause harm in your Life Sciences classroom. How would you prevent this?
