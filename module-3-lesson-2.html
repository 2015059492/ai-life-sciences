Lesson 2

Module 3: Designing AI-Enhanced Lessons (Using TPACK & UDL Simply)

In this lesson, you will put everything you have learned into practice by designing your own AI-enhanced lesson or classroom activity. Using simple prompts and templates, you can create materials that support your learners’ understanding and engagement. No advanced skills are needed — just your knowledge of Life Sciences and the tools introduced earlier.

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, teachers will be able to:

  1. Design a simple Life Sciences lesson or activity that incorporates AI tools.

  2. Apply TPACK and UDL principles in your lesson design.

  3. Use AI to support different learner needs and enhance understanding.

Step-by-Step Guide to Creating an AI-Integrated Lesson

  • Step 1: Choose the Topic – Select a Life Sciences topic you want to teach.

  • Step 2: Identify Learning Goals – Decide what learners should know or be able to do.

  • Step 3: Pick AI Tools – Decide which AI tool will help explain, visualise, or simplify content (e.g., ChatGPT for explanations, Diffit for simplified reading materials, or AI diagram generators).

  • Step 4: Plan Your Lesson Flow – Outline your lesson introduction, teaching, practice, and assessment sections.

  • Step 5: Differentiate Using UDL – Provide multiple ways for learners to understand and engage with the content.

  • Step 6: Review and Adjust – Check if the AI content aligns with CAPS and is clear for your learners.

  1. Using Templates to Help
    You can create a simple template:

    • Topic: __________

    • Learning Objective: __________

    • AI Tool Used: __________

    • How Learners Will Engage: __________

    • How Learning Will Be Assessed: __________

    This makes lesson design quick and structured.

  2. Examples

    • Genetics: Use AI to generate a pedigree diagram and a simple explanation. Provide three versions for different learner levels.

    • Photosynthesis: AI can generate a step-by-step diagram, and learners can answer questions generated by AI about each step.

  3. Tips for Low-Resource Classrooms

    • Use AI to prepare worksheets or explanations in advance.

    • Print diagrams or explanations for learners without devices.

    • Encourage group work and discussions around AI-generated content.
