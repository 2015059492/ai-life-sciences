Module 5: Final Exam

Congratulations! You have reached the final part of this course. In this module, you will demonstrate your understanding of AI integration in Life Sciences teaching by completing a final project/exam. The project will ask you to create a lesson plan or activity that integrates AI responsibly, aligned with CAPS and ethical guidelines. This is your opportunity to apply everything you’ve learned in a real teaching scenario.

Lesson Objectives

By the end of this lesson, teachers will be able to:

  1. Design a Life Sciences lesson or classroom activity that integrates AI tools effectively.

  2. Apply ethical principles in AI use, including privacy, bias awareness, and copyright considerations.

  3. Align AI-supported activities with CAPS curriculum requirements.

  4. Demonstrate critical thinking by reflecting on AI’s role in supporting learner engagement and understanding.

  5. Use AI to enhance lesson planning and differentiation for diverse learner needs.

Exam Instructions

  1. Task: Create a Life Sciences lesson plan or classroom activity that incorporates AI tools. Your submission should include:

    • Topic and grade level

    • Learning objectives (aligned with CAPS)

    • AI tools used and how they support teaching

    • Ethical considerations and responsible use of AI

    • Differentiation strategies for diverse learners

    • Reflection on how AI enhances learning

  2. Submission:

    • Complete your final lesson plan or activity using any format you prefer (Word document, PDF, Google Doc, etc.).

    • When you are ready to submit, upload your answer sheet using the link below:
      Upload exam here

    • Make sure your file name includes your full name and module, for example:
      Name_Surname_AI_Final_Project.pdf

  3. Assessment:

    • Your submission will be assessed using a rubric that evaluates:

      • Alignment with CAPS objectives

      • Appropriate and responsible AI integration

      • Clarity and practicality of the lesson plan

      • Consideration of ethical issues and learner needs

      • Creativity and engagement

  4. Tips for Success:

    • Review examples from previous lessons to guide your plan.

    • Ensure your AI-generated content is accurate and ethically used.

    • Keep your lesson practical and realistic for your classroom context.
